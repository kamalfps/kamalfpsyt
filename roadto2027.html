What your data says

  • Short clips hold people: Clove clip was watched ~65% through, Jett ace ~72%. Channel average is only 40 seconds per view.
  • Stream viewers stay ~10 minutes when they arrive, so make 8–10 minute highlight videos from long streams.
  • Result titles gain subs: “Reached Silver” got 4 subs from 28 views.
  • Hinglish question titles have your best CTR (9–12%).

Every day, same routine

  1. Stream with a title that has a question or result plus the day number.
  2. Say your goal and ask for one subscribe in the first 30 seconds.
  3. Cut 3 Shorts (15–30 seconds, best moment in the first 2 seconds).
  4. Make a thumbnail in the studio tab.
  5. Log your subs on the Home tab.
